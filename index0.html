About

How I work

Concise on the page, thorough in the work — I separate verified data from estimates and flag what still needs confirmation.

Research-first, always

I start with primary sources — SEC 10-K filings, shareholder letters, Nielsen and Deloitte industry data — then build to a recommendation. Every Adobe and Netflix report I wrote follows a nine-section format: business, products, competitors, growth, profitability, future plans, stock performance, price projection, and a clear call.

Numbers with judgment

Valuation isn’t just EPS, P/E, and PEG. I’ve learned when those metrics break down — loss-making companies, GAAP distortions, negative earnings — and how to say so honestly instead of forcing a multiple to fit. That judgment is what turned research into buy/sell picks for a real portfolio.
